Bio for immediate release

EVERFINER is a Boston based six-piece ensemble rocking thoughtful originals in overlapping genres including, shimmer-folk, shimmer-funk, soul, and pop. BIG HUGE harmonies lay on top of a tight, minimalist rhythm section, and if you're lucky a horn section will casually be lowered from the rafters.

Lyrically these tunes work through issues of identity, boundaries, long distance relationships, short distance situationships, and the flux of family.

EVERFINER is a beautiful collective of musicians who care as much about each other as they do about their art. As we all grow and learn each day we are all becoming a little more EVERFINER.

Musical influences: Trousdale, MJ, Sam Cooke,
Lake Street Dive, The Beths, Sammy Rae & The Friends, PUP, Kimbra.
